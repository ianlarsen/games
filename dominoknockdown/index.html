  1. Draw. Use a finger, Pencil, or mouse to draw one continuous line. Lift and continue from its end. Or choose one of five preset paths; selecting a preset replaces your drawing.
  2. Ready. Your chosen surface appears with classic black dominoes and bridges at crossings.
  3. Go. Travel just ahead of the cascade, or enjoy it from above.

Cross your line to create a bridge. Choose Wood, Stone, Lava, or Dirt before pressing Ready. Lava paths sit on a stone ledge. Undo removes the last stroke. Your drawing is saved on this device.

Gentle motion keeps the camera overhead and slows the cascade. You can stop a ride at any time.
