## Labeled clarifications

- REM is distinct from stage 1 NREM; skeletal muscle tone normally drops greatly. Avoid the screenshot’s old stage-transition wording and implication of repeated gross muscle activity. [NIH: Sleep stages](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep/stages-of-sleep).
- Opponent processing follows cone responses, rather than being literal pairs of color receptors. It complements trichromatic theory. [OpenStax: Vision](https://openstax.org/books/psychology-2e/pages/5-3-vision).
- Taste receptor cells are in taste buds, many of which are within papillae. [OpenStax: Other senses](https://openstax.org/books/psychology-2e/pages/5-5-the-other-senses).
- Sensory relay through the thalamus has the familiar exception of initial olfactory processing; the blanket screenshot wording is not used as an exception-free question. [OpenStax: Brain and spinal cord](https://openstax.org/books/psychology-2e/pages/3-4-the-brain-and-spinal-cord).
- Gate control concerns modulation of pain transmission, not a literal ranking of important messages. [Melzack & Wall, 1965](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/5320816/).
- The cerebral cortex’s fixed 1 mm thickness and developmental neuron count are not assessed; these generalizations are not needed to learn its identity and connections.
- “Nerve deafness” is rendered as sensorineural hearing loss. Hair-cell damage is the tested example; the concept can also involve the auditory nerve.
- The GABA problem cell is unclear (“internalizes…”). It is excluded from grading, rather than guessed. GABA’s inhibitory role is retained. [OpenStax: Cells of the nervous system](https://openstax.org/books/psychology-2e/pages/3-2-cells-of-the-nervous-system).
- The table’s neurotransmitter/disorder links are labeled as associations in the supplied table. They are not universal causal statements or diagnostic rules. [NIMH: Depression](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/depression).

Scientific clarifications were checked October 24, 2026. They improve the wording of the supplied concepts without adding an unseen curriculum. Every table messenger’s function is covered; clear disease associations are covered with source-specific wording. GABA’s unclear problem cell is the deliberate omission.
