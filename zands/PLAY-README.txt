Z & S PLAY — SIX-GAME MOBILE EDITION

Open index.html. Use the blue Main screen button to return to the games.
Pick Zion or Samaria for separate saves. On a phone, Audio controls music
and effects separately. Tap saved artwork to download it.

Includes Paint Studio, Picture Remix, Match Quest, Number Quest,
Pattern Beats, and Shape Builder. Balance Dash and Marble Lab are removed.

Tangrams click and turn into place near their matching spaces.
Pictures have sixteen unique tiles and one exact completed arrangement.
Eight improved scenes are included for painting and remixing.
Music is softer, arranged in stereo, and stored as compact MP3 files.
